# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

We are committed to providing a friendly, safe, and welcoming environment for all contributors regardless of background, identity, or experience level.

## In short

- Be respectful
- Critique ideas, not people
- Assume good faith
- Help newcomers

## Reporting

Suspected violations can be reported to **conduct@handshake.ai**. Reports are handled confidentially. The response process follows the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.

For the full text of the Contributor Covenant v2.1, see: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/
